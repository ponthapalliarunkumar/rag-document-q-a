Company Leave Policy

1. Annual Leave
Employees are entitled to 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, accrued monthly. Unused annual leave of up to 5 days may be carried forward to the next year; any excess will lapse.

2. Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for sick leave exceeding 2 consecutive days.

3. Casual Leave
6 days of casual leave are provided per year for short-notice personal matters. Casual leave cannot be combined with annual leave for more than 3 consecutive days without manager approval.

4. Maternity Leave
Eligible employees are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave, in accordance with applicable labor laws.

5. Paternity Leave
Eligible employees are entitled to 10 days of paid paternity leave, to be availed within 3 months of the child's birth.

6. Public Holidays
Employees are entitled to all officially declared public holidays as per the company holiday calendar, published at the start of each year.

7. Leave Application Process
- Leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 3 working days in advance (except sick/emergency leave).
- Approval is subject to manager sign-off and team workload considerations.
- Unapproved absences will be treated as leave without pay (LWP).

8. Leave Without Pay (LWP)
Employees may request unpaid leave in exceptional circumstances, subject to HR and management approval, for a maximum of 30 days per year.

9. Encashment
Eligible unused annual leave may be encashed at year-end as per company policy, subject to a maximum cap defined by HR.

For further clarification, employees should contact the HR department.
